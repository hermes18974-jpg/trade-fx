STRATEGY RULES
FX RICH SMC Strategy - Top-Down Framework ▼
1. Top-Down Analysis Flow
Weekly → Daily → 4H → 1H → 15m. Identify institutional bias on HTF first, then drill down for precise entry. Never trade against the HTF trend.
2. The 4/5 Confirmation Rule
A trade setup requires at least 4 out of 5 confirmations to be actionable. 3/5 is a "watch" — close but not yet tradable. Below 3 is NO ENTRY.
3. Confirmation: TREND_ALIGNMENT
EMA stack alignment (9/21/50) confirms directional bias. BULLISH: EMA9 > EMA21 > EMA50. BEARISH: EMA9 < EMA21 < EMA50.
4. Confirmation: HIGH_VALUE_ZONE
Price must tap a valid supply/demand zone (FVG or order block). Zone must have minimum ATR% width (0.3%).
5. Confirmation: LIQUIDITY_SWEEP
Sweep of PDH/PDL or PWH/PWL shows liquidity grab. "Smart money" taking stops before reversing. Sweep + rejection = powerful entry signal.
6. Confirmation: LTF_TRIGGER
Lower-timeframe price action trigger: pin bar (body ratio >= 0.35), engulfing candle, or BOS on 5m/15m after all HTF confluences align.
7. Confirmation: EXTRA_CONFLUENCE
Fib zone (0.618 or 0.786 retracement), round number level, or session overlap (London/NY). Adds probability weight.
8. Risk Management
Risk per trade: 2% of account equity. Max 5 open positions. SL always defined before entry. Position size = (Account x Risk%) / |Entry - SL|. Timeout: 8 candles.
